## Climate

- USDA hardiness zone 8b (roughly -9 °C / 15 °F winter lows in a bad year;
  most winters bottom out around -3 to -5 °C).
- Köppen Csb: dry warm summers, wet mild winters. Annual precipitation
  ~1,000–1,200 mm, most of it October–April as long light rain, not storms.
- Winter days are short (~8.5 h daylight at solstice) and overcast. Solar
  charging for a sensor node is marginal December–February; plan for a
  battery that can carry 3–4 weeks without meaningful sun, or run mains.
- Summer: July–August are dry; forage dearth from late July into September.
  Water source near the hives matters in that window.
- Convergence-zone weather: Woodinville sits near the Puget Sound
  Convergence Zone, so localized heavy showers and wind gusts are common.
  Hive stand must be level, heavy, and strapped.

## Forage (approximate timing for the Sammamish valley / Snohomish border)

| Window | Source | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Feb–Mar | Hazel, willow, crocus, early maple | Pollen; colony build-up starts |
| Apr | **Bigleaf maple**, dandelion, fruit blossom, vine maple | First real nectar flow; can be heavy in a dry April |
| May | Cherry laurel, hawthorn, holly, cotoneaster, raspberry | Steady |
| Jun–mid Jul | **Himalayan blackberry** | *The* main flow. Most of the surplus honey comes from here. |
| Jul–Aug | Clover, fireweed (if nearby), Japanese knotweed (Aug–Sep) | Knotweed is an invasive but a real late nectar source |
| Sep–Oct | Ivy, asters, sedum | Ivy honey crystallizes hard; fine for the bees as winter stores |

## Regulations

- **WSDA apiary registration** is required in Washington for all beekeepers
  (RCW 15.60). Register annually; the hobbyist fee is small. Do this before
  bees arrive.
- **City of Woodinville / King County**: confirm current zoning rules on
  hive count, setbacks, and flyway barriers for the parcel. Record the code
  section and date checked here: ____
- Local clubs worth joining for mentorship and nuc availability:
  Puget Sound Beekeepers Association (Seattle), Northwest District
  Beekeepers Association (Snohomish County), Washington State Beekeepers
  Association.
