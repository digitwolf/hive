# Seasonal calendar — Puget Sound lowlands

Generic PNW beekeeping calendar, to be tuned with our own logs over time.
Dates shift ±2 weeks year to year; watch the maple and blackberry bloom, not
the calendar.

| Month | Colony | Beekeeper tasks | Monitoring signal to watch |
|-------|--------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| **Jan** | Tight cluster, queen may start laying late month | Don't open. Heft/scale check for stores. Clear entrance of dead bees. Check roof/strap after storms. | Weight trend (stores consumption ~0.5–1 kg/wk). Cluster temp ~20–30 °C rising when brood starts (~34 °C). |
| **Feb** | Brood rearing starts; stores consumption jumps | Emergency feed fondant/dry sugar if light. Order packages/nucs **now** — they sell out. | Weight drop accelerating = brood. Watch for weight < ~15 kg stores. |
| **Mar** | Build-up; first pollen | First quick inspection on a >13 °C calm day. Check queen (eggs), stores, disease. Start 1:1 syrup if light. | Foraging flights on warm days; humidity should fall as cluster expands. |
| **Apr** | Rapid growth, maple flow | Add second brood box when 7–8 frames covered. Swarm prevention begins. **Install new bees** (packages early Apr, nucs late Apr–May). | Weight gain on maple days. Sound: strong daytime hum. |
| **May** | Swarm season peak | Inspect every 7–10 days for swarm cells. Add Flow super on strong colonies once second brood box is ~80% drawn. First mite wash. | Sudden weight drop of 1–2 kg in an hour = swarm. Piping/quacking in audio = virgin queens. |
| **Jun** | Blackberry flow starts mid-month | Super space! Second mite check. | Daily weight gains of 1–3 kg possible. |
| **Jul** | Flow ends late Jul; dearth begins | **Harvest** Flow frames only when fully capped (check windows and by pulling a frame). Mite wash → treat if >2–3 mites/100. Robbing screens. | Weight plateaus then declines. Entrance activity drop. |
| **Aug** | Dearth; varroa peak; "winter bees" reared now | **Mite treatment** (Formic Pro or Apiguard; temp constraints). Reduce entrances. Remove Flow super if not harvesting. | Temp in super vs brood nest. |
| **Sep** | Ivy/knotweed; queen slows | Finish treatment. Feed 2:1 syrup to reach ~25–30 kg stores. Remove Flow super; store frames. | Weight climbing on feed. |
| **Oct** | Final prep | Mouse guards on. Insulation + moisture quilt on. Tilt hive slightly forward for drainage. Strap. Consider OA treatment when broodless (late Oct–Dec). | Baseline winter weight logged. |
| **Nov–Dec** | Cluster | Leave alone. Oxalic acid dribble/vapor on a broodless day. Check for wind damage. | Weight, temp, and *humidity* (>80 % RH in the top box = condensation problem). |

## Regional temperature constraints for treatments

- Formic Pro: 10–29 °C daytime highs → fits Aug–Sep here, avoid heat waves.
- Apiguard (thymol): >15 °C → Aug–early Sep.
- Oxalic acid: any temp; most effective broodless (late autumn).
- Apivar (amitraz): any temp; 42–56 days, not during honey supers on.
